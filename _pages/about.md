# Hi there!

I am a cardiologist and echocardiographer at St. Paul's Hospital in Vancouver, a Clinical Assistant Professor in the Division of Cardiology at the University of British Columbia, and Director of the Providence Health Care Cardiology Digital Solutions/AI hub. I completed my postdoctoral research and advanced echocardiography training at the University of California, San Francisco. I trained in internal medicine and cardiology at the University of British Columbia, where I was also a fellow in the Clinician Investigator Program and a Friedman Scholar. I hold an MD from McMaster University and a Master of Information and Data Science from the University of California, Berkeley.

I am working on applications of machine learning and computer vision for cardiovascular disease phenotyping and prognostication, particularly focused on echocardiography. I enjoy working on statistical analysis of large datasets within medicine, deep learning, multimodality, and development and validation of predictive AI models. For more info about what I have worked on, please see the [list of my publications](/publications).
